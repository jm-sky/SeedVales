## Audit of shared problems (before changes)

1. **Conflicting cast.** In H two packs filled the same single SM slots with different people: hunter (Jarosław / Mara), guard (Wojciech / Ada), woodcutter (Mirosław / Bram), trader (Stanisław / Oren). Pack C used "Olek" as a buyer (Q11) and a quartermaster (Q12); "Anika" kept two different cemeteries; "Tomas" (Q13) clashed with "Tomasz" (G08), "Marta Ash" with Marta (G03); Mira (G01) and Mara (Q01) were confusable.
2. **Mismatch with the generator.** The home settlement is SM and has no blacksmith, yet G02 put smith Bogdan in H. Q09 assumed a poor trader although traders start with 200–400 c. Q02/Q08 happen in V but used H's guard. Q09 put a V farmer into an H barter. Q10 sent pioneers from H to an outpost days beyond T. "Domowice" isn't in the settlement name pool.
3. **Accidental blocks.** Pack B's global `roadActive` blocked 5 quests; packs A and C didn't know it. G04 counted only herbs gathered after the start (`questTagged`).
4. **Repetition.** Three wolf quests (Q02, G03 background, G07) with two wolf dens by roads; the three treasure quests of pack C shared one skeleton (cemetery → record → ruin → animal → buyer → sell/restore/partnership); four different "rich buyers" with a sale ending; Q01/Q04/Q09/Q11–Q13 ended in confirming sums.
5. **Paperwork over adventure.** Packs A and C: "Show me the payment", "witnessed count", a licence for a sword, ledgers and signatures in nearly every scene. Q05 (a "treasure map") gave only a family ring — an expedition without a real reward.
6. **Dialog.** Packs A/C: strings of aphorisms ("Excitement is a poor shovel", "Memory is not a contract", "Time is a material too"), meta-comments ("because a quest marker is patient", "start the progress bar, as you people say", "The route is not found because the player has arrived at a marker", "a name is not a stat bonus"), the same "X is not Y" construction. Pack B: single lines of full exposition, mechanics described in NPC mouths, no conversation.
7. **Theme constraints.** G06 built its choices on secretly opening an entrusted letter (betrayal of trust); G03 had blackmailing a child's mother; G08 had planting false evidence against the neighbouring village; G01 had an unmotivated "give the lamb to Wanda" branch.
8. **Logic and causality.** G06 "warn Janko" about a deal Janko is part of; G03 "report" paid the same as empathy; G08 "truth" punished Tomasz −20; G01's thief had no believable excuse; Q02's guard paid "for the work" from no source; Q06 collected a "hammer" confusable with Q04.
9. **Rewards.** No scale: small quests and treasure expeditions paid similarly little, or "substantial" without numbers. Calibration: [QUEST-WORLD.md](QUEST-WORLD.md#reward-calibration-proposal).

## Decisions and assumptions (explicit)

- **D1.** Merged cast per [QUEST-WORLD.md](QUEST-WORLD.md). Renaming is not cosmetic — it resolves slot conflicts. *Second pass:* English first names + occupational surnames (author).
- **D2.** `roadActive` removed; replaced by deadline warnings (N). *Second pass:* the author left this open; the reviewer kept it removed because it created cross-pack blocks.
- **D3.** Dialog uses `{H}/{V}/{T}`; prose uses H/V/T.
- **D4.** *First pass:* languages kept as they were. *Second pass:* all quest documents in English (author: better consistency and AI work). Historical review files stay in their original language.
- **D5.** Mechanic status codes: **I** implemented (verified in FEATURES), **P** planned, **D** deferred, **N** new proposal.
- **D6.** Q02: wolves → sow with farrow (variety; APPENDIX: aggression near young). Q11: boar → bull moose (marsh). Q13: moose → rutting stag.
- **D7.** Bernard Smith (old Bogdan) moved to V and merged with the smith's father (Tobin); G02's direction reversed (smith in V, debtor is H's common field).
- **D8.** No new quests, no game code, no FEATURES changes. The in-game name pool and settlement names are still Polish — flagged as a follow-up in [QUEST-WORLD.md](QUEST-WORLD.md#casting-rules), not changed.

## Log: problem → fix (per quest)

Each quest: R1 story/motives/causality, R2 dialog/voices/rhythm, R3 playability/states/endings/consistency. R1 and R2 were done while rewriting each file (separate checklists: causality and NPC knowledge, then voices/aphorisms/meta-comments). R3 was a check of the finished text right after writing (states, reachable endings, payers, I/P/D/N codes) — fixes listed below; files were not each re-read from disk separately. No playtests and no implementation. The second (author-decision) pass changed names and language only, plus small consistency fixes listed at the end.
