# App review, round 1 (review--001, application part)

- **Round:** 1 of at most 3. **Scope:** the whole app: gameplay loops, graphics, UX on desktop 1280×720 and the mobile viewport. Authored quests Q03, Q07, G03 and G01 (QUEST-03) are walked through the UI for the first time.
- **Commit under review:** `95bd76e` (main). The review scripts were changed on top of it (no `src/` changes): `28754b4` (review-quests walks the authored quests) and `6cfb839` (review-quests accepts its own warehouse notice and records the nest rats that are left; review-ui sets a real illness kind).
- **Reviewer:** an independent Opus subagent in an isolated worktree (skill `app-review`).
- **Evidence:** `pnpm review:app` ran with exit 0: 193 screenshots, 0 console errors, every step performed. review-quests was run again after `6cfb839`. Two throwaway probes ran against the same dev server: (1) the rats quest with every nest rat killed, which completes; (2) `px.visited` and the guard's daily schedule. Screenshots are in `test-results/review/<scenario>/` (gitignored); 20 copies are in [assets/016/](assets/016/).
- **Caveats:** headless SwiftShader, so no FPS or GPU claims (D-PERF-2). The scenario scripts pause the sim and set state through `__sv`, which produces three artifacts. They are **not** findings: "not visited" on the map (the sim is paused before `navigationSystem` runs; the probe shows `visited [0]` after 10 s unpaused), the stale toasts, and the bow shots at a fleeing animal 80 m away.
- **Legend:** ✅ confirmed · 🟡 assumption · ❓ open (❓ user = a taste or design call for the user).

### Observations rejected or confirmed as working

- **Guard "Sleeping" at 10:00: rejected.** Guards are on night duty and sleep from 09:00 to 16:00 (`npc/goals.ts:136-137`). Probe schedule for the Maplewick guard: 09 Patrolling · 11/13 Sleeping · 15/17 Patrolling · 19 Lighting torches · 21/23 Patrolling at night · 01 Going for a meal · 03 Feeding the fire. One possible ❓ remains: the dialog shows "Now: Sleeping" for an NPC the player is talking to.
- **Authored quests are completable end to end through the UI**: talk → topic → options → journal (J) → world objective → reward. Money is conserved in all four (world money 3985 constant). Rewards come from the stated sources:
  - Q03: 10 c from Miles's purse (31 → 21), opinion +15 for the household, Helpfulness +4.
  - Q07: Mark's torch (torches 2 → 3), opinions +10/+5, Helpfulness +3.
  - G03: 15 c from the treasury (150 → 135), Honesty +5, Helpfulness +10, Hazel +20 / Martha +15 / Mark +20.
  - G01: 10 c to Piers, 25 c back from Molly's purse (36 → 11), 2 wool, Helpfulness +5; Piers removed, nothing held.
  - Q07 timings: dusk hold, 6 posts lit with E, 2 roast batches of 0.5 h each with the pan.
  - G03 night scene: Hazel snapped 1.7 m from the dark post at 01:16.
  - Natural offer at day 2: only G01 ("The lost lamb" from Peter Herder); the log says "Peter Herder has something on their mind." at 07:00, which helps discoverability.
- **Needs (idle, per game hour)** match `calibration.ts`: hunger 3.35, thirst 5.0, vigor 2.5, social 2.8. Well: thirst 40 → 85 in 2 s. Bread: +25 satiety. Inn: 8 c, vigor 35 → 98 after about 5.7 h.
- **Build and fire numbers match the blueprints:** campfire 0.24 h, 3 branches = 2.1 h of fuel; hearth 0.48 h, lights with 3 starter branches = 2.1 h, +0.7 h per branch; shed 1.9 h + 3.7 h; torch 5 h. Failure texts are clear: missing materials, needs a shovel, too close, in water, uneven ground.
- **Bandage** +15 HP and stops bleeding; the herbalist charges 15 c for +55 HP, −8 h of convalescence and stopped bleeding.
- **Fog of war:** teleported frames show a black minimap (unexplored), and the map lists only known settlements. No leak seen.
- **Language:** no non-English hits in `ui-checks.json`, and no Polish text seen in any screenshot.

### Not checked

- Audio.
- Real touch gestures: joystick drag, camera swipe, long press. Mobile panels were opened by taps on testids.

- The other endings and branches of the authored quests, quest timeouts and Mark's held schedule over days; only the main paths were walked.
- Save/load during a quest.
- Rain/winter gameplay effects beyond the frames.
- Carts and companions in the field.
