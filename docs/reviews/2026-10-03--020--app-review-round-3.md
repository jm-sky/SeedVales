- **Reviewer:** an independent Opus subagent in an isolated worktree (skill `app-review`).
- **Evidence:**
  - `node scripts/e2e/review-run.mjs` (the 7 existing scenarios) exited 0. - **Caveats:**
  - Headless SwiftShader, so no FPS or GPU claims (D-PERF-2). Sound cannot be heard headless. Only sound requests, failed loads and the settings were checked.

  - In the `jump` step the recorded `midY` equals the ground height because `sim.player.y` was sampled at the wrong moment. The screenshot shows the player clearly in the air with the shadow below, so the jump works. The e2e acceptance step 17 covers it.
