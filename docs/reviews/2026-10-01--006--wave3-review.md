## Details

### 1. A hungry companion switches between `eat` and `follow` (Medium, ✅)
- **Where:** `src/game/sim/npc/goals.ts:103` (`eat` → `go(door)` + `eat_store`), `goals.ts:126-130` (`follow` is offered only when d > `followM`), `src/game/sim/npc/ai.ts:143` (forced replan at d > 1.5×`followM`).
- **Description:** A companion only eats in place when it carries food (`findFood(h.inv)`). Otherwise `eat` plans a walk to the household door, which can be kilometres away. When the companion is within 3.5 m of the player, `follow` is not offered and `eat` (score ≥ 0.02) beats `wait` (0.12) as soon as hunger < ~60. The NPC walks off. At 5.25 m the decision-cadence check forces a replan and `follow` (0.72) beats `eat` + 0.15, so it comes back. Once it is within 3.5 m again, the cycle restarts. At hunger < 15, `eat` scores about 1.06, beats `follow` (0.95), and the companion walks home and leaves the player.
- **Failure scenario:** Hire the son for 3 days and walk 400 m from his home. Within a few game hours he oscillates visibly every ~2 s and never eats. Later he abandons the player to eat at home. D-NPC-3 says "needs keep running; away from home the companion camps", but only sleep has an "away" variant.
- **Repro (✅):** Hire the son, remove the food from his pack, walk the player 480 m east, then hold `hunger = 45` and later `30` for 180 s. Result: **90 goal switches in 180 s**, max distance 6.3 m, trace `0.2s:eat@3.5m 2.2s:follow@5.5m 4.0s:eat@3.6m …`.
- **Fix direction:** Add an "away" branch for companion needs, as sleep already has. Eat from the pack, otherwise ask for or take food from the player (a gift), otherwise buy from the nearest settlement. Never walk to a home store more than ~150 m away while the contract runs, or end the contract explicitly ("I'm going home to eat"). Add a test: a companion with hunger 30, 500 m from home, keeps the same goal for at least 30 s.

### 2. `follow` re-plans on every tick (Medium, ✅)
- **Where:** `ai.ts:145-146`. `follow` is planned with `steps: []` (`goals.ts:128`), so `ai.stepIdx >= ai.steps.length` is always true. That calls `planNpc(sim, h, force=true)` on every `updateNpc`.
- **Description:** While following, which is the steady state of a companion, the AI evaluates `goalOptions` every update. That means near-LOD ≈ 10 Hz, with `sim.actors.query(…, 150)` for downed neighbours, `threatNear`, scoring noise from `sim.rng`, and `plan()`. Hysteresis is skipped because `force` is true. This is the root cause of the instant `follow → eat` flip in #1. It also goes against the decision-cadence design (AI-01 / D-SIM: decisions about every 1 s) and PERF-01. `fight` has the same pattern, but it is short-lived. `follow` lasts the whole trip.
- **Repro (✅):** `updateNpc` called alone on a hired son while the player walks at 1.5 m/s. Result: **247 `ai.plans` counted in 247 follow ticks** (one plan per tick).
- **Fix direction:** Give `follow` a sentinel step, or exclude `'follow'` (and `'fight'`) from the `stepIdx >= steps.length` replan trigger. Replan at the decision cadence or `REPLAN_S` instead. Add a test: `ai.plans` while following ≤ ~1 per second.

### 3. Free opinion farming through buy and sell (Medium, ✅)
- **Where:** `src/game/sim/trade.ts:108` (`npc.opinion + 1` on every purchase), the `Math.max(1, …)` minimum price in `buyPrice` and `sellPrice`.
- **Description:** Selling one branch and buying it back costs 1 c each way, so the net is 0 c, and every purchase adds +1 opinion. This existed before for the trader only. TRADE-02 makes it work with every NPC, and opinion now drives hire consent (`hireRefusal`), `joinChance`, guards leaving their post (≥ 40), and the wish dialogue.
- **Repro (✅):** Woodcutter, 60 sell+buy cycles of a branch. Money delta **0**, opinion **0 → 60**.
- **Fix direction:** Scale the opinion bonus with the money spent, give it at most once per day per NPC, or give none for buying back what you just sold (for example, a `boughtFrom` marker).

### 4. Buy-low / sell-high arbitrage (Medium, ✅, older formula now reachable through every NPC)
- **Where:** `trade.ts:82` (`scarcity` 0.9 when stock > 10) and `trade.ts:93` (sell multiplier capped at 0.9, buy multiplier floored at 0.95).
- **Description:** With high mood the buy price is price×0.9×0.95 = 0.855×price, and the sell price is up to 0.9×price. Once a household holds more than 10 of an item, the player earns money by buying and reselling the same stack. The player can push the stock above 10 by selling items first, and can raise mood with #3 for free. Money is conserved (the NPC pays), but an NPC's purse and, through taxes, the treasury can be drained without limit.
- **Repro (✅):** Farmer, opinion 100, A = 0.9, player trade skill 60, 12 leather boots in the store. Buy 15, sell 16, so **+20 c for the player and −20 c for the NPC over 20 cycles**.
- **Fix direction:** Make sure sell ≤ buy − 1 for the same item and NPC, for example `sell = min(sell, buyPrice - 1)`, or compute the sell multiplier from the same mood term so it can never cross the buy price. Add a regression test that loops buy+sell and expects the money delta to be ≤ 0.

### 5. Helpfulness loop through the cart and the warehouse (Medium, ✅)
- **Where:** `src/game/sim/cart.ts:109` (+1..3 helpfulness per unload ≥ 10 c), `interact.ts:474/496` (no penalty for taking once helpfulness ≥ 10).
- **Description:** The single-stack deposit loop already existed (+1). The cart triples the gain and makes it one click. Load, unload into the warehouse, take back, repeat. There is no cost once helpfulness reaches 10.
- **Repro (✅):** Handcart, 5 iron ore, helpfulness 10, 10 loops: **10 → 30**, and the ore is back in the pack.
- **Fix direction:** Give goodwill only for net contributions. Track a per-settlement `donated − taken` value balance and reward only increases of that balance, or cap it per day. The same applies to `transferToStorage`.

### 6. Trade works at any distance, including the companion's home store (Medium, ✅)
- **Where:** `trade.ts:18` (`tradeInventory`), `trade.ts:39` (`tradeStock` uses `houseOf(…).inv` at any distance).
- **Description:** A companion is always next to the player. When trading with it, the player buys from its household store and sells into it, kilometres from home. That works as an instant remote stash: sell heavy goods to the companion on the road and buy them back at home, with no carrying. The same applies to the market `trade` option, which opens trade with the settlement's trader even when the trader is away as a companion.
- **Repro (✅):** Hired son placed 3803 m from home. `tradeStock` lists the store stacks `log×4, branch×16`.
- **Fix direction:** When the NPC is more than ~150 m from its house (or is a companion), use only `npc.inv` for both stock and destination. For the `market` option, require the trader to be within a few metres of the market.

### 7. A bow can be used while pushing a cart (Low-Medium, ✅)
- **Where:** `src/game/sim/loadout.ts:42` (`switchWeapon`, no cart check), `Game.ts:364`, `player.ts:133` (bow draw does not check `px.cart`).
- **Description:** `attack`, `useItem` and the torch are guarded, but the weapon-switch hotkey is not. It puts a two-handed bow in hand and holding primary fires it.
- **Repro (✅):** Push a wheelbarrow, `switchWeapon(sim, 'ranged')`, draw for 2 s and release. Result: `In hand: Short bow`, the cart is still pushed, arrows 10 → 9, 1 projectile.
- **Fix direction:** Add a single `handsBusy(sim)` check used by `switchWeapon`/`setPrimary` (or park the cart first, as `equip()` in `runOption` does) and by the bow draw in `playerSystem`.

### 8. Nearly rotten raw meat roasts into safe meat (Low, ✅)
- **Where:** `src/game/sim/cooking.ts:46` (`ROAST.minFreshFrac` 0.2), the eating rule in `actions.ts:370` (spoiled < 15% of `spoilH`).
- **Repro (✅):** Raw meat with `fresh` 0.2 h (spoiled threshold 2.7 h) gives cooked meat with 14.4 h (threshold 10.8 h), so it is **not** spoiled and has no extra illness chance.
- **Fix direction:** Keep the spoiled state: if the raw meat is spoiled, the cooked meat should be too (for example, floor at `minFreshFrac` only when the raw fraction ≥ 0.15). Alternatively, set `minFreshFrac` below the 0.15 eating threshold. D-FOOD-3 states the 20% floor, so ❓ this needs an author decision on intent.

### 9. `companionsOf` scans every NPC once per companion per tick (Low, 🟡)
- **Where:** `src/game/sim/npc/companions.ts:26,171` (`followPoint` → `sim.state.npcs.filter`).
- **Description:** O(N NPCs) per companion per update, which goes against PERF-01's literal rule. The cost is small (N ≈ a few hundred, at most 3 companions).
- **Fix direction:** Keep a `companionIds` list (derived and rebuilt on hire, dismiss, death and load), or cache it per tick.

### 10. `follow` never gives up when stuck (Low, 🟡)
- **Where:** `companions.ts:185` (`steerTo` result checked only for `'arrived'`).
- **Description:** When the player crosses deep water or climbs a cliff, the companion keeps steering every tick (forced by #2). No `failGoal` and no cooldown, unlike `fight` (`ai.ts` `failGoal(…, 0.25)`).
- **Fix direction:** On `'stuck'`, wait (switch to `wait` for N seconds) or use the navigation fallback, and tell the player ("X can't follow you here").

### 11. A gifted melee weapon is not wielded by an NPC holding a bow (Low, 🟡)
- **Where:** `companions.ts:129`.
- **Description:** COMP-03 says a received better weapon "is actually used". It is used only at ≤ 6 m in `fight`, through `wieldBest('melee')`. That is probably acceptable, but no test covers it.

### 12. No test for the v5 → v6 migration (Low, 🟡)
- **Where:** `src/game/save/migrate.ts:57`. `save.test.ts` builds its v1 fixture from a current snapshot, so `carts` is already there and the backfill never runs.
- **Fix direction:** In the SAVE-01 test, `delete st.carts` and expect `m.carts` to equal `[]`.

- 14: RES-07 raised the stone yield of boulders (see the table). If unintended, set `ROCK.chunkStones` to 2, or 3 to give a small reward for the extra break step.

## Checked, no issues found
- **Conservation:** roasting removes exactly the number of raw pieces it produces (the batch is capped again at completion, inputs are taken only at completion, so interrupting is safe). Cart load and unload move whole stacks through `removeStack`/`addItem` with no duplication, `stowCart` refuses a non-empty cart, and `pushFromPack`/`stowCart` move the item 1:1. Rock pieces: each strike drops exactly one chunk until `left` reaches 0, and `breakChunk` consumes one chunk for `ROCK.chunkStones`. Gifts move the stack player → NPC pack. Hiring moves coins 1:1 (`totalMoney` unchanged, tested).
- **Saves:** `px.cart`, `state.carts`, `ItemStack.sp`, `Human.{kin, companion, gifts, joinAskDay}` all live in `GameState` and survive the JSON round trip. Migration 6 → 7 (`kin`) is tested. The extra son uses a separate RNG stream. The ids of entities created after the sons (the player) shift, which has no effect on old saves because the population is stored in the save.
- **Layering:** `check-layers` OK. `render/carts.ts` only reads sim. The sim imports no three/vue.
- **Fog of war:** no new map or minimap elements (carts and companions are not drawn on the map).
- **Contract expiry and return home:** after `dismissCompanion` the NPC goes back to normal goals and walks home. ✅ A companion dismissed 3.8–4.4 km from home (the farthest settlement or land point on seed 1337) got back within 51 m / 33 m after 1 h of gameplay with the player far away (far LOD). The far-LOD `> 5000 m` failGoal rule cannot trigger on this seed (max land distance 4.4 km), but it could on larger worlds or seeds.
- **Hire then dismiss:** paid up front with no refund, so there is no profit. `askToJoin` allows one roll per NPC per day. At most 3 companions.
- **Threat handling:** `threatNear` with `ward` (animals targeting the player) uses a spatial query. A companion's `defends` follows D-NPC-3.
